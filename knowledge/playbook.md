# Panel playbook

What the panel has learned about *how to discuss*, promoted by `scripts/improve.py` from observer
notes when the same tagged pattern or shared pitfall appears in two different rounds. Every expert
reads this file at session start, alongside `facts/`, `hypotheses/` and `methods/`. Cite an entry
as `P-001`. Entries are one line, one rule, with the rounds that earned them. Cards stay
source-based; cross-expert lessons live here.

Format: `- [P-NNN] (YYYY-MM-DD, kind: pattern|pitfall, tag: <tag>, source: <session rN; session rN>) <rule>`

## Entries
- [P-001] (2026-11-24, kind: pattern, tag: challenge-changed-number, source: 000807.SZ/2026-11-23-05 r1; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-02 r1) Challenge the figure a colleague underwrites, not the narrative; when the challenge lands, switch figures and record who conceded. Example: Net the gross margin against the selling ratio before calling a cost line a cut: when costs are reclassified the two move in opposite directions and the sum is what changed.
- [P-002] (2026-11-24, kind: pattern, tag: hypothesis-names-verifier, source: 000807.SZ/2026-11-23-05 r1; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-02 r1) State a hypothesis together with the document or series that would settle it and when it can be checked. Example: State what would change your mind as a dated, numeric test, and put a cost or balance you cannot explain into open questions instead of guessing its cause.
- [P-003] (2026-11-24, kind: pitfall, tag: multi-idea-turn, source: 000807.SZ/2026-11-23-05 r1; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-02 r1) Pitfall: more than one idea or more than three figures in a turn. Seen as: three-figure cap broken: t7 about 16 figures, t1 about 11, t6 about 10
- [P-004] (2026-11-24, kind: pitfall, tag: required-return-unargued, source: 605499.SH/2026-11-23-02 r1; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-03 r1) Pitfall: r [or g, tax] taken as given and never argued although the answer depends on it. Seen as: the 8–10% hurdle is stated as an assumption and never argued although the whole implied-growth range depends on it
- [P-005] (2026-11-24, kind: pattern, tag: peak-earnings-normalized, source: 000807.SZ/2026-11-23-05 r1; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-03 r2) Refuse a trailing multiple for a cyclical or a windfall margin; bracket a normal band and test the low end against the hurdle. Example: Normalize a cost windfall in the margin before rerunning the reverse curve, so a borrowed cushion becomes a number the price must earn back.
- [P-006] (2026-11-24, kind: pattern, tag: disagreement-narrowed, source: 000807.SZ/2026-11-23-05 r1; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-03 r3) Narrow a real disagreement to the missing fact and name the document or series that settles it; do not paper over it. Example: Concede what the record supports and isolate the unexplained sequence as the remaining question.
- [P-007] (2026-11-24, kind: pattern, tag: operating-financing-separated, source: 605499.SH/2026-11-23-03 r1; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-03 r3) Take financial assets, debt and their income out of operations before any return or valuation. Example: Parked cash is worth about face value and its carry is excluded from core profit; deployment, not the raise, is the swing factor for value.
- [P-008] (2026-11-24, kind: pattern, tag: reconciliation-derivation, source: 605499.SH/2026-11-23-02 r1; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-03 r3) Reconcile two sources or two experts' numbers with a bridge that closes before reading either. Example: Decompose a falling ROE into operating return and cash drag before calling it franchise decay.
- [P-009] (2026-11-24, kind: pattern, tag: scorecard-first, source: 000807.SZ/2026-11-23-05 r1; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-04 r1) Open round one with the business verdict off the full ten-year record, before any recent event. Example: Open round one with the business verdict off the full record [great/good/gruesome and the one scorecard line that surprised you] before any recent event.
- [P-010] (2026-11-24, kind: pattern, tag: source-passage-applied, source: 605499.SH/2026-11-23-03 r2; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-04 r1) Read the source passage in the turn and apply its test to this company, labelling the result plausible or proven. Example: Read the See's passage in the turn and compare its capital-need pattern to this company's sales-versus-NOA record.
- [P-011] (2026-11-24, kind: pitfall, tag: assumption-as-finding, source: 605499.SH/2026-11-23-02 r1; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-03 r2; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-03 r3; 605499.SH/2026-11-23-04 r1) Pitfall: an estimate or scenario spoken as a fact.
