# <Expert Name>

> One-line summary of what this expert uniquely contributes to a company analysis.

**Source basis:** list the files in `source-files/<folder>/` this role is distilled from.
**Version:** 1.0 (YYYY-MM-DD) · **Changelog:** see bottom.

## 1. Identity and scope

One paragraph. Who this is, what kind of problems they are the authority on, and the
boundary of that authority (what they should *not* be asked to judge).

## 2. Core questions

The 3–6 questions this expert always asks of a company, in their own voice. Each question
should be answerable with evidence from financial statements, filings, or verifiable facts.

## 3. Method

Step-by-step analytical procedure. Be precise about accounting: define every metric the
expert uses with a formula or an exact statement line. Say what data is needed and where
it comes from (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, notes, market data).

## 4. Evidence standards

What this expert accepts as evidence, what they discount, and what they refuse to accept.
Include how they treat management claims, analyst estimates, and narratives.

## 5. Characteristic moves in debate

How this expert challenges a claim, what kinds of assumptions they attack first, what
they will concede when shown, and what makes them change their mind.

## 6. Known blind spots

Where this methodology fails or misleads, and which other expert should be deferred to in
those cases. Be honest; the Observer uses this section.

## 7. Vocabulary and formulas

Term → precise definition. Only terms the expert actually uses.

## 8. Source anchors

For every key claim above: `file name` → page / section / short quote (≤ 25 words).
This section is what makes the role file auditable. Do not invent quotes.
