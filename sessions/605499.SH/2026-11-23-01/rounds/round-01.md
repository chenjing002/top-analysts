### Stephen Penman

**Position.** The S1 quote (20261120, price 113.55) does not price a permanent end of growth. Turn the price into value and ask what it needs. On the brief's FY2025 operating base, the price needs about 2% perpetual residual-income growth at an 8% required return (derived: reverse curve, see evidence). That is a market paying for a business that grows near inflation, not for a boom and not for zero. I cannot say the market is *over*-discounting, because there is no 2026 profit data.

**What we know versus speculation.** The reformulated book of operations is almost nothing: NOA is 6.4 (S2 reformulated_bs, FY2025), and 99% of the 693.66 EV is premium (derived: (693.66-6.4)/693.66). The book anchor therefore says little here. I switch to the forward-earnings anchor. TTM net income is about 48.0 (derived: 833.68/17.37) against 44.15 for FY2025, which is +8.7% (derived: 18.88/17.37). TTM revenue is 226.28, +8.4% on FY2025 (derived: 185.96+40.31 vs 208.75). So TTM earnings growth matches TTM revenue growth, and margin is intact at about 21.2% of revenue (derived: 48.0/226.28). A normal P/E at r=8% is 12.5 (S2 valuation anchors), against 17.37 TTM. The premium of roughly 28% of market cap is the growth being paid for (derived: 1-12.5*48.0/833.68).

**Reverse curve at the S1 quote** (derived: EV-NOA = ReOI0(1+g)/(r-g), OI 41.0, NOA 6.4). Implied g is 0.1% at r=6%, 2.0% at 8% and 3.9% at 10%. Growth-at-risk, solved the other way (derived, same inputs): at g=0 the price offers a 5.9% return, at g=2% 8.0%, and at g=4% (a GDP-type ceiling) 10.1%. At the 20251231 close the same method needed 4.7% growth at 8% (S2 reverse_curve, brief). The market has already removed about 2.7 points of growth from the price, which is consistent with a deceleration it believes, and does not look like a collapse to zero.

**Decomposing the deceleration.** Q3 growth of 0.8% sits against a +30.4% comparison base, and Q4 2025 was +22.9% (derived: 40.31/32.81). The quarterly path (21.5%, 11.3%, 0.8%) is a fall in the *rate* of growth. It is not a decline in the level of revenue. Inventory is the warning: 6.65 at 20260930 against 5.52 a year earlier is +20.5% (S1 period_trend, derived) while Q3 revenue is flat. That is a build, not a sale.

**Cash and advances.** Customer advances (合同负债) were 59.74 against 47.61 a year earlier, +25.5% (S1 balance sheet FY2025/FY2024). They are operating liabilities that fund NOA at no interest, and advance days rose to 93.8 (S2 working_capital). CFO/NI was 1.40 (S2 cash reconciliation). So FY2025 earnings were conservative in cash terms, but this is the first number to fall if distributors stop pre-paying. I have no 2026 contract-liability or cash-flow figure. That gap decides whether this is a pause or an unwind, and I will not guess it.

**Assumption challenged.** The brief's reverse curve mixes definitions and dates: EV uses a narrow financial-asset set (S1 EV items, 20260930) that leaves out 债权投资 17.57, 其他非流动金融资产 7.12 and 一年内到期的非流动资产 13.79 (S1 balance sheet FY2025), while NOA 6.4 and OI 41.0 are the broader S2 FY2025 figures. Deducting those items from EV (my approximation, about 38.5, derived) lowers implied g at 8% from 2.0% to about 1.7%. The effect is small, but the curve is not like-for-like. Treat the 2.0% as an upper bound.

**Share count.** Use market cap, not price. Shares went from 5.20 to 7.342 (+41.2%), market cap fell 40.0% and price fell 57.5% (derived). The 7.342/5.20 ratio of 1.412 is not a clean split ratio, so a bonus share, a placement or both are possible, and the fact sheet does not say which. A placement would put new cash into the 833.68 cap with no FY2025 earnings behind it, which would inflate the 18.88 FY2025 P/E relative to a per-share view.

**Confidence: medium, capped.** The curve rests on S2 defaults (r 8%, g 3%, tax 25%) and an FY2025 OI base. Over-discounting specifically is low confidence.

**What would change my confidence:** Q3 2026 and 9M profit (归母净利润), operating cash flow and 合同负债: advances still growing with flat revenue would raise confidence toward 'pause'; advances falling would move it toward 'unwind'; The cause of the share count rising from 5.20 to 7.342 亿股, and whether the equity raise explains the cash rise of about 87 亿 (the brief's figure); A like-for-like reformulation at 20260930 (all financial assets in NFO, NOA rebuilt) in place of the FY2025 NOA/OI base; Evidence on distributor inventory and sell-through (channel checks are not in the sheet), to tell a pause from lost shelf share; A hurdle return from the investor in place of the S2 default of 8%

**Assumption I would challenge:** The brief's reverse curve assumes that EV (narrow financial-asset definition, S1 20260930), NOA 6.4 and OI 41.0 (broad definition, S2 FY2025) belong in one equation. They do not share a definition or a date, so the 2.0% implied g at r=8% should be read as an upper bound (about 1.7% when like-for-like financial assets are deducted). A bull framing that treats 17.37x TTM as cheap because 'growth will re-accelerate' also needs to show that TTM earnings, up about 8.7%, are still growing faster than the 2% the price needs.

### Jeff Bezos

**Position.** The cash statements describe a high-conversion business whose conversion has leaned on a growing customer float. A growth pause does not break that model, but it removes a cash source I would not capitalise. I cannot call this a permanent end of growth or a pause from the price alone.

**Earnings versus cash.** CFO/NI was 1.40 in FY2025, 1.74 in FY2024 and 1.61 in FY2023 (S2 cash reconciliation). But FCF simple fell to 39.1 from 41.0, down 4.6% (derived: 39.1/41.0-1), while net income rose from 33.3 to 44.1 (S2 cash reconciliation). Earnings up, free cash flow down: that divergence is my primary finding.

**The float.** 合同负债 (contract liabilities) rose 12.13, from 47.61 to 59.74 (S1 full balance sheet). That is 19.6% of CFO (derived: 12.13/61.74) and 69% of the 17.6 gap between CFO and NI (derived: 12.13/17.6; S2 cash reconciliation, accruals). Float growth of 25.5% in FY2025 trailed revenue growth of 31.8% (derived from S1 full balance sheet and S1 annual 营业收入). In FY2024 it was 82.6% vs 40.6%, FY2023 60.2% vs 32.4%, FY2022 31.1% vs 21.9% (same derivation). So FY2025 is the first of the four years I can compute in which the float grew slower than sales. Year-end float per yuan of revenue slipped from 30.1% to 28.6% (derived: 47.61/158.39; 59.74/208.75).

**Does the float need growth?** The cycle is -13.2 days before advances and -107.0 after (S2 working capital). The structural negative comes from advances, not supplier paper: 应付票据 (notes payable) is nil in FY2025 (S1 full balance sheet). If the advances are dealer prepayments tied to expected sales (my inference; the annual report was not read), a flat top line freezes the float near 28.6% of revenue and removes the 12.13 contribution. CFO would be about 49.6, CFO/NI about 1.12 on FY2025 earnings (derived: 61.74-12.13; /44.15). A revenue decline would unwind it: each 10% fall at a constant ratio releases about 6.0 (derived: 59.74 x 10%). The quarterly path 21.5%, 11.3%, 0.8% (S1 quarterly revenue) is therefore a working-capital signal as well as a sales signal.

**Capital intensity.** Capex was 22.7 against D&A of 5.0, 4.58x (S2 capital & reinvestment), and 在建工程 (construction in progress) rose from 5.54 to 14.14 (S1 full balance sheet). That capacity was sized in years of 30-47% growth (S1 annual 营业收入, FY2024-25). If growth stalls, the 14.14 is a machine awaiting its one-machine NPV, which I cannot run because unit economics are not in the sheet.

**Cash against borrowing.** Financial assets were 207.18 against 短期借款 (short-term borrowings) of 62.73 at 20260930 (S1 EV items). At 20251231, cash plus trading assets of 119.89, less float of 59.74, less borrowings of 66.30, is -6.15 (derived, S1 full balance sheet). Net of the float, the surplus is not obviously a surplus. The carry looks positive (pretax net financial expense of -4.1, i.e. net income; S2 reformulated income statement), so I do not read distress, but restriction or dealer-deposit status is unknown.

**Price.** At the S1 quote of 20261120 (113.55; market cap 833.68), the brief's reverse curve implies g of 0.1% at r=6%, 2.0% at 8% and 5.8% at 12% (derived, FY2025 base, S2 method; I re-derived the 8% point as 1.99%). That prices deceleration, not collapse, but valuation belongs to the valuation expert. The share count of 7.342 vs 5.20 is unexplained, so I state no FCF per share.

**What would change my confidence:** Cap: anything resting on S2 defaults (r 8%, g 3%, 25% tax) is at most medium; the reverse-curve point is also on a FY2025 base with no 2026 profit data, so the overall verdict stays low.; 2026 Q1-Q3 CFO, capex and 合同负债, or the FY2026 year-end float: a float that is flat or shrinking while revenue is flat would confirm dependence on growth; a float still rising would support a pause.; The annual report note on 合同负债 (dealer prepayments versus consumer advances, and any deposit or restriction terms) and on 货币资金 restrictions.; A clear explanation of the share count rise from 5.20 to 7.342 亿股 (bonus issue, placement or other) so FCF per share can be built.; Evidence on unit economics of the new capacity (capex per incremental unit of sales, utilisation) to run the one-machine NPV.

**Assumption I would challenge:** That the ~207 亿 of financial assets is free surplus that can be netted against debt in the EV bridge (NFO -140.44). Part of that cash is funded by the customer float (合同负债 59.74 at FY2025), and the company still carries 62.73 of short-term borrowings. Net of float, FY2025 surplus is about -6.15 (derived), so the bridge may overstate shareholder-owned cash.
