## Verdict
The round answered the first half of its question and correctly declined the second. "The S1 quote does not price a permanent end of growth" holds on the panel's inputs. I reproduced implied g of 1.99% at r=8% (0.09% at 6%, 3.90% at 10%). The panel also refused to call the stock over-discounted without 2026 profit, operating cash flow or 合同负债. The overall rating of `low` is earned. The stated `medium` for "not a zero-growth price" is allowed by the S2-default cap, but the headline is stronger than the curve supports. At r=6% the implied g is 0.1%, and Penman's own growth-at-risk line shows g=0 returns 5.9%, so the claim depends on a hurdle of roughly 6% or more. The conclusion gives the curve points but states the headline unconditionally. Rebuttals were strong at catching errors: the Li Lu slip was caught by three experts and conceded, and Penman and Buffett each corrected their own bases. The weaker parts are these. About half the rebuttal effort went to a side question (the 5.20 to 7.342 亿股 share count), and the inference that a cash issue explains the cash rise rests on a numerical coincidence plus an unverified dividend assumption. Several challenges (Penman on TTM growth, Penman to Bezos on the EV bridge) were never answered. All four openings exceed the 250-600-word limit.
