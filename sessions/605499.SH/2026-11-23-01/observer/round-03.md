## Verdict
The round answered its question: the 2026 share increase, profit, cash flow, advances and a like-for-like reverse curve were all restated from local quarterly tables. Every numeric claim I checked reproduces. The headline "not a zero-growth price, about 1% at r = 8%" is a fair reading, and overall `low` confidence is earned. The weaker parts are interpretive. (1) The "3-for-10 bonus issue" is stated as fact in the conclusion although Li Lu said the issue type is open, and only the ratio 1.30 supports it. (2) The "core" operating profit adds back financial expense, which is the item that swung most, and it is the sole basis for "profit held up". (3) The 25% Q3 CFO fall is a single-quarter reading that the nine-month data do not support. (4) The 1.0% vs 4.7% comparison mixes NOA conventions. The `medium` confidence on single turns is mostly fair. The selling-expense insight is a good lead but remains a hypothesis.

## Weaknesses
- [severity: medium] Bonus issue stated as fact. Conclusion.answer says "a 3-for-10 bonus issue in Q2 (type, date and buyers not in local data)" and the split-adjusted -45% rests on it. Evidence is only 7.342/5.648 = 1.30 and the capital reserve falling 2.65 at Q2. The data would equally fit a multi-part placement. A bonus issue of 1.69 亿 shares at par 1 yuan would take about 1.7 off the reserve, not 2.65, and c_recp_cap_contrib also rose 0.42 in Q2. The numbers would be consistent with the conclusion, but not proof. — Penman turn 1 ("looks like"), conclusion.answer — keep the conditional form from turn 1 in the conclusion. If it is a bonus, the 45% figure follows; otherwise the per-share fall would be about 58%. Li Lu raised the limit but no one tested it.
- [severity: medium] Core operating profit definition. "operate_profit − (fv + invest) + fin_exp" was chosen by Penman after he suspected the financial lines (turn 3: "I suspect that is the financial lines"). It is not neutral. Reported operate_profit fell 5.7% in Q3 (16.76 vs 17.78). Of the +1.05 core gain, 0.58 comes from adding back the fin_exp swing (+0.49 vs -0.09) and 1.47 of non-core decline is removed. Interest income on 207 亿 of cash is inside the financial line, so that cash earns nothing on this basis but its cost to hold it is also removed. Other income, impairments and government grants stay inside. The headline "core +6.5% on 0.8% revenue" and Li Lu's concession both depend on it. Calling financial expense "a cost of the proceeds" is also the hypothesis (Penman turn 3 challenge), not a finding: the panel itself said FX or debt cost cannot be separated. — Penman turn 3; Li Lu turn 7 concession — show reported, core and an alternative (reported op profit growth, and core with fin_exp left in: 16.12 to 16.68+... about 3.0%) side by side before conceding.
- [severity: medium] Q3 CFO "down 25%" is one quarter. Bezos turn 5: Q3 10.49 vs 13.93 (-24.7%, reproduced), but Q2 2026 was 20.41 vs 11.09 (+84%) and nine months are 35.42 vs 31.33 (+13%). The Q3 fall may be timing against a Q2 surge, so "cash is the cleaner witness" is not shown. The disagreement log and open_questions repeat "Q3 CFO fell" with no working-capital bridge (tax, payables, advances). — Bezos turn 5 — use the nine-month or TTM view, and note the 2026 dividend paid (34.56 in 9M, 1.43 in Q1 against 26.78 in FY2025) as a cash-flow timing item.
- [severity: medium] Comparison of 1.0%-1.3% (2026) against 4.7% (2025) mixes bases. 2025 uses S2 reformulation NOA 6.4 and OI 41.0. The 2026 base uses OI 45.9, a panel-defined core TTM, and NOA of 10 or 41. NOA 41 equals B less (cash and trading assets less short-term borrowings), which is the same method as NOA 6.4 at FY2025 on my reading of the turn-5 note, so "NOA is not pinned down" is overstated and the 10 case looks arbitrary. The "3.5 points fall" in the text is therefore a mix of price fall, different OI definition and NOA rise. The ReOI0 in 2026 also takes tax at 21.8%, taken from 9M2026 not tested. — Penman turn 5 — rerun 2025 with the same core OI definition, or restate the 3.5-point statement as indicative.
- [severity: medium] The headline curve is hurdle-dependent (carried from round 1). At r = 7% g is 0.0% to 0.3%, at 6% it is negative. "Not a zero-growth price" holds only if r > 7%. The conclusion gives the range −0.9% to 3.3%, but the answer sentence still leads with 1.0%-1.3% at the S2 default 8%. — conclusion.answer — add the hurdle at which g = 0 (about 7%).
- [severity: low] Li Lu turn 7 is muddled. A "weak 40.31 亿 Q4 2025 comparison base" helps a bull (easy comparison: Q4 2026 at Q3's 61.5 would be +53%), so it does not support the bear case. Buffett's gross-margin comment (turn 4) says Q3 "did not widen in Q3 as in Q2", but Q3 GM 45.7 is +0.5 pt year on year. His own number does not show a weakening. — Li Lu turn 7; Buffett turn 4 — state the mix point and that the Q4 base effect cuts for growth.
