## Disagreements

- **Great or good?** — narrowed
  - Warren Buffett: Great for the existing business, good while it keeps building capacity
  - Li Lu (李录, Himalaya Capital): Cash conversion is flattered by dealer prepayments (about 60 cents of profit as free cash flow without them); a great label needs the float to hold
  - _resolution_: Both accept high returns and low upkeep capital; they differ on whether dealer prepayments are a durable feature of the franchise. The year-end 2026 balance would settle it.
- **Does the book protect the downside?** — resolved
  - Stephen Penman: Net cash is 17–18% of the price on either date and the rest is plant and earnings power
  - Li Lu (李录, Himalaya Capital): Even a generous floor is 19–21% of the price, so the downside is real if profit falls
  - _resolution_: Agreed that the book is not a floor on either basis (FY2025 year-end plus the raise: 15–19%; September 2026: 16–21%); the haircut, the prepayment deduction and the pro forma raise are assumptions.

## Conclusion

**Answer (medium confidence):** It is a great franchise priced as if growth ends. Sales grew almost seven times since 2018 with almost no net capital added, because dealers prepay about 60 亿 a year ahead; the return is a margin story, with net margin up from 7% to 21%. Cash backs profit, but about a quarter of eight years of operating cash was dealer float; without it free cash flow was 60% of profit. At 113.55 the operating business is 15 times its after-tax operating profit (a 6.7% yield), which needs only about 1% to 3% perpetual growth on either basis, and book value is no floor: net cash is 17–18% of the price and a conservative floor 15 to 21%, on either balance sheet (FY2025 year-end plus the 99 亿 raise, or September 2026). Before the raise the year-end floor was only about 3% of today's price. Scorecard: business type great with a float caveat; cash backing about 100% with the float and 60% without; return driver margin; cheap only if the franchise lasts; floor 15–21% of price.

**What would change our mind**
- Year-end 2026 dealer prepayment balance does not build by about 20 亿 or Q4 sales miss 43 亿 (float reverses: great → good)
- The announcement shows the 99 亿 is going into low-return uses (capital allocation worse)
- Electrolyte and tea margins stay at 35% or lower while energy drink growth stays near 7%

**Open questions**
- What is the 99 亿 for, and on what terms was it issued? Needs the issue announcement.
- How much of capex is upkeep? Needs the annual report capex note.
- What are the 18 亿 of non-current assets due within a year at Sept 2026 (13.8 at FY2025), and the 17.6 of debt investments and 7.1 of other financial assets at FY2025? Needs the notes; they are outside the 140 亿 core net cash.
- Is the user an owner or a one-year holder?
- Why does FY2025 net cash plus the raise (148.6 亿) exceed the September balance (140.0 亿) by 8.6 亿? Needs the nine-month cash flow walk.
