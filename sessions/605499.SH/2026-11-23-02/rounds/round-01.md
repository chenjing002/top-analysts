That is a demand or shelf problem, not a price war, and I trust it more than a margin story. I'd still watch how often the company needs promotions to get the bottle out.

### Warren Buffett

Careful with 'marketing down 1.1'. Marketing was 17% of sales in Q1 and Q2 and only 13% in Q3, and gross margin jumped to 49.7% in Q2. Those two move in opposite directions. Take them together and the margin is 32% in both Q2 and Q3, against 30% last year. Over nine months, marketing is actually higher than last year, 16.0% against 15.5%. So the company did not cut marketing; it moved the cost around. The gain is about gross margin, 47.5% against 45.2%.

## Disagreements

- **Do dealer prepayments (+43% yoy, flat since June) show a pause?** — open
  - Jeff Bezos: Yes, dealers are not cutting orders.
  - Li Lu (李录, Himalaya Capital): No, prepayments lag sell-through; the year-end figure is the test.
- **Should the 99 亿 raised be charged an 8% return?** — narrowed
  - Stephen Penman: Yes, it is part of equity; 2.75% implied growth.
  - Warren Buffett: No, it is idle cash; about 2%.
  - _resolution_: Both numbers are shown; the use of the money decides it.
- **Was Q3 profit helped by a marketing cut?** — resolved
  - Stephen Penman: Marketing fell 1.1 亿.
  - Warren Buffett: Marketing and gross margin offset; nine-month ratio is up.
  - _resolution_: Timing between two lines; profit gain is margin.

## Conclusion

**Answer (low confidence):** The price does not assume growth ends: at 8% it needs about 2.75% perpetual growth in residual earnings (about 2% if the new cash and its interest are excluded), against 5.0% at year-end. Whether Q3 is a pause or the start of a lasting slowdown cannot be settled from one quarter; the panel leans to a pause on dealer prepayments and margins and stays unsure because growth has slowed three quarters in a row. The profit gain is gross margin, not a marketing cut.

**What would change our mind**
- Q4 2026 revenue below the 40.31 亿 base
- Year-end dealer prepayments well below the 59.74 亿 of last year
- A 2026 announcement showing the 99 亿 is not held for use in the business

**Open questions**
- What is the 3.55 亿 of non-interest financial cost in nine months of 2026?
- What will the 99 亿 be used for?
- Bonus issue or placement behind the move to 7.34 亿 shares, which is not in local data
