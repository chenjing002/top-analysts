## Conclusion

**Answer (low confidence):** The 2026 capital record is mostly owner-minded but carries one unanswered 99 亿 question. The company raised about 99 亿 in the first quarter at an implied price below last year's close, then parked roughly 55 亿 net in wealth management while plant spending fell about 16%, and in the same nine months declared about 36 亿 of dividends (87% of profit) and bought back about 10 亿 of stock. The confusing second share step was a free bonus issue, not dilution. The borrowing next to the cash pile is a rational positive carry — about 1.4% cost against 2.6%-plus yields — not distress. The raise does not change the operating valuation: the cash is already excluded from the operating enterprise value, parked cash is worth roughly face value, and the growth the price needs stays at the modest ~1.8% at an 8% hurdle. What the raise does is dilute reported return on equity from about 52% to about 34%, which is a growth problem (more cash than the business can absorb at its old return), not a quality problem. Read together with rounds 1 and 2 — cooling growth, a borrowed margin cushion, and now a cash-bloated capital base — this is three independent signs that the easy compounding is over, and the market already prices modest growth rather than the old 28% compounder. The unresolved governance point is the purpose of the raise: the use of proceeds is not in the local data, and an exchange loss hints at possible foreign-currency proceeds. Until the issue announcement is read, management is owner-minded on the evidence available, with a 99 亿 question mark. Confidence: medium on the cash-flow facts and the carry/ROE reading, low on the governance verdict and the carry hypothesis.

**What would change our mind**
- The issue announcement earmarks the 99 亿 for a specific high-return, pre-sold project: the raise is foresight, upgrade management.
- The announcement shows no stated use and the cash stays parked: raise-then-return is share-price management, stay cautious.
- The buyback price (not local) proves below intrinsic value: the repurchase was disciplined, not just generous.
- Cash capex reaccelerates above the depreciation run-rate with a clear payback: the business is absorbing capital again.

**Open questions**
- What was the 99 亿 raised for — use of proceeds, and is it an A-share placement or an offshore/H-share listing? Needs the issue announcement/prospectus.
- Loan terms behind the ~1.4% borrowing cost: is the carry confirmed or a reading of the interest lines? Needs the interim report borrowing note.
- The ~3.55 亿 of other financial cost and the −2.08 亿 exchange loss: what are they? Needs the interim report financial-cost note.
- Buyback price and purpose, and whether the treasury shares will be cancelled. Needs the buyback announcement.
- Does the 21.81 亿 interim dividend signal a payout-policy shift? Needs the dividend announcement.
- 2026 share prices are not local (S2 daily ends 20251231), so the buyback's price discipline cannot be judged.
