## Business scorecard (the panel reads this)

### 1. What kind of business? — GOOD in harvest; the first half of the decade was GRUESOME

- Operating profit (营业利润) 2.6 (FY2016) → 86.5 (FY2025), ~33×; core pretax OI 11.1 → 88.9, ~8×.
  Revenue 155.4 → 600.4, ~3.9×. [S2 raw annual; S2 reformulated_is]
- Capital it needed: fixed assets (固定资产) 174.8 → 203.3 (+16%); NOA 170.5 → 254.3 (+49%).
  Working capital is supplier-financed (OWC negative every year). [S2 raw_balancesheet; reformulated_bs]
- But the path splits the decade:
  - FY2016–20: parent net profit Σ 7.0; capex Σ 167.3; FCF Σ −47.8; three parent placements
    (2016: 36.3; 2019: ~21.1; 2021: 30.0 — Σ ≈ 87.4), shares 26.07 → 34.68亿 (+33%).
    Growth required repeated injections at inadequate returns → gruesome phase.
  - FY2021–25: parent net profit Σ 223.1; capex Σ 42.6; FCF Σ 308.7. Growth stopped needing
    capital (capex cliff 52.9 → 12.7 → 6.4); cash left for owners → good/harvest phase.
- No pricing power: aluminum is a globally priced commodity; the profit is a spread
  (aluminum price − alumina − power). Cost position: hydropower-based smelting; FY2025 RNOA 28.6%
  vs 中国铝业 15.7%, 神火 13.8% [S2 peers]. Capacity ceiling / hydro advantage are policy facts
  not verifiable in local data — labelled assumptions where used.
- Verdict: **good, not great** — attractive returns now need little new capital, but the return
  level is cycle-dependent, not franchise-protected. (GGG savings-account test, SRC read this round.)
