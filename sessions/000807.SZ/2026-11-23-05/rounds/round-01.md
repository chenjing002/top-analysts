## Conclusion

**Answer (medium confidence):** 云铝股份 is a good, harvest-phase commodity business, not a great franchise. The decade splits in two: a gruesome 2016–2020 build-out (7亿 parent profit on 167亿 capex, three placements totaling ~87亿, shares +33%) and a 2021–2025 harvest (223亿 profit on 43亿 capex, net debt of 134亿 turned into net cash of 212亿). The 2026 profit spike (9M parent 116亿; gross margin 10%→32% in four quarters) is cost-driven — revenue +19% while cost of sales fell 4% — consistent with an alumina-price-collapse hypothesis, and the market knows it: the stock fell 36% YTD to 21.01 even as TTM earnings tripled. So PE(TTM) 5.5 and a 21% FCF yield are peak mirages; on mid-cycle profit of 55–60亿 the price is 12–13×, implying ~1% perpetual growth at an 8% hurdle (zero-growth return 7.5–8.2%), with net cash equal to 29% of market cap as cushion and an asset floor near 36% of cap. Verdict: reasonably cheap on normalized earnings, not on peak; the reader should remember that the price already pays for the bust, not the boom. The swing facts are alumina-price persistence and whether the 232亿 cash hoard reaches owners. Scorecard: business type — good in harvest, commodity spread not franchise; cash backing — 10y FCF 261亿 = 1.13× parent NI, but current FCF overstates owner cash if maintenance ≈ D&A; return driver — margin 0.7%→10.1% and turnover 0.49→1.36 on a shrinking base, leverage gone (multiplier 3.5→1.4); cheap/floor — priced at mid-cycle no-growth, floor ~264亿 protects about a third. Confidence: medium (normalized-earnings assumptions, 25% tax default, unverified controller/related-party facts, no local commodity price series).

**What would change our mind**
- Alumina price rebound or evidence that alumina oversupply is resolving — compresses the spread toward the 50亿 EBIT low end
- Related-party note showing alumina purchase pricing away from market, or a policy-driven mega-investment/acquisition of the 231.8亿 hoard
- Annual-report disclosure that true maintenance capex (pot relining, 大修) is far above current capex — cuts owner earnings below reported NI
- A 2026 dividend cut, or recurring Yunnan power rationing / tariff hikes that structurally raise the cost position
- Evidence the 2026 margin spike is volume/mix-driven rather than cost-driven (would raise the normal band)
